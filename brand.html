Brand & Press

The files and the words to use when you show The Stoop.

Logo files, colors and type, a boilerplate you can paste, the share image and the founder's headshot.

This page is for partners, press and event organizers who need to show The Stoop, whether that's a sponsor board, an article or a speaker slide. Download the files, paste the boilerplate and use the mark as it is. If you need something that isn't here, ask.

The Mark

The bars lockup is the only current mark: three yellow bars rising next to THE STOOP in Anton. Use the white version on dark backgrounds and the black version on light ones. Don't recolor it, stretch it or add effects, and leave clear space around it.

Colors and Type

Yellow on black, with white text. The grey is for panels and borders.

  • Yellow#FFCC00
  • Black#0B0B0A
  • White#FFFFFF
  • Grey#1A1A1A

Two typefaces: Anton for headlines, set in capitals, and Outfit for everything else. Both are open source and free from Google Fonts.
