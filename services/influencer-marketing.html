Who It's For

This service is built for brands in fitness, wellness, food & beverage, DTC, and apparel who want to scale through authentic influencer partnerships. Whether you're launching your first influencer campaign or looking to professionalize and scale an existing program, we handle the end-to-end workflow so your team can focus on product and growth.

Most of our clients are in the Pittsburgh area, and because most of the work happens digitally, we run campaigns for brands nationwide too. For X Shadyside, a family-owned fitness facility in Pittsburgh’s Shadyside neighborhood, we sourced 50+ local creators, activated 15 across two seasons, and drove 267K+ views and 5.8K+ link clicks (how 15 fitness influencers drove 267K+ views). For Dr. Tea, a bubble tea shop in the same neighborhood, the #LoveIsBrewing Valentine’s Day campaign, built around two local creators we brought into the shop, generated 480,334 views at a $4 CPM (how we generated 480K views). We also brokered a three-way partnership across X Shadyside, SXP, and Lucid Juice (our cross-brand partnerships guide), and we measure campaigns on CPM, CPC, and engagement rate, not follower count (how to calculate influencer marketing ROI).

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you find and vet influencers?

We source influencers based on audience demographics, engagement rates, content quality, and brand alignment. Every influencer is vetted before activation — we review their content history, audience authenticity, and past brand partnerships.

What platforms do you run influencer campaigns on?

We primarily execute on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and Facebook. Platform selection depends on your target audience and campaign goals.

How much does an influencer campaign cost?

Influencer campaigns typically range from $2,000 to $15,000+, depending on the number of influencers, deliverables, and usage rights. For X Shadyside, 15 influencers across two campaigns drove 267K+ views on $6,750 total spend, about $0.025 per view. Per-influencer rates by follower tier, and what each campaign includes, are on our pricing page.

How do you measure influencer campaign success?

We track views, impressions, engagements, link clicks, CPM, CPC, and conversion events. Every campaign ends with a detailed post-mortem report with actionable insights.

How do you find local influencers in Pittsburgh?

We screen for local reach: creators based in your market or with strong local engagement, because a national influencer means little to a neighborhood business. Each one is vetted for audience fit, engagement quality, and brand alignment before activation. For X Shadyside, we sourced 50+ Pittsburgh-area fitness, lifestyle, and student creators across two seasons and activated 15.

Micro-influencers or mid-tier influencers: which works better for a local business?

For a local business, a creator’s local audience matters more than follower count. Our two X Shadyside campaigns mixed creators with 1.2K to 101K followers, and micro-influencers (1K–25K followers) with local credibility outperformed larger accounts. Per-activation rates for micro and mid-tier (25K–100K) creators are on our pricing page.

How long does an influencer campaign take?

Our X Shadyside campaigns each ran across two to three calendar months: “New Year, New Us” from December 2022 to February 2023, and “Fall into Fitness” from August to September 2023. Within a campaign, we recommend staggering posts across 4–6 weeks rather than 1–2, because sustained visibility outperforms a single burst.
