FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is influencer coaching for?

Influencers at any stage — whether you're just starting to explore brand partnerships or you're already landing deals but know you're undercharging.

What does a coaching session cover?

Sessions cover pitching brands, negotiating rates, building media kits, personal brand strategy, content monetization, and career planning — tailored to where you are.

How much can I expect to earn from brand deals?

Rates depend on your platform, niche, audience size, and engagement. Our coaching helps you understand your value and price accordingly.

Is this 1-on-1 or group coaching?

We offer 1-on-1 personalized coaching sessions. Each session is tailored to your specific goals, platform, and experience level.
