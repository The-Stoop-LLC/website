The Challenge

Sylvan Gardens had built a strong reputation through word-of-mouth and quality craftsmanship — but their social media presence didn't reflect the caliber of their work. They needed a partner who could manage daily social content, showcase their landscape projects, spotlight team members, and build an engaged community on Facebook and Instagram.

IG Reach vs. Prior Year

Over the first nine months of management, The Stoop delivered a combined 38,000+ total impact across Facebook and Instagram and produced consistent, professional content that showcased Sylvan Gardens' craftsmanship.

The team spotlight series and project showcases became the highest-performing content formats, with posts like 'Meet Jesus,' 'An Incredible Stone Patio Transformation,' and 'Meet the Green Team' driving the strongest engagement. Monthly analytics reporting gave Sylvan Gardens clear visibility into what was working and where to double down.

Monthly Highlights

  • April 2024 — 7,578 total impact, 977 IG accounts reached
  • May 2024 — 7,693 total impact, 1,400 IG accounts reached (peak reach)
  • June 2024 — 4,902 total impact, 535 IG accounts reached
  • Q2 2024 subtotal (Apr–Jun) — 20,173 combined impact, 2,912 IG accounts reached
  • August 2024 — 4,034 total impact, 734 IG accounts reached
  • September 2024 — 3,488 total impact, 898 IG accounts reached
  • October 2024 — 4,432 total impact, 807 IG accounts reached
  • November 2024 — 2,692 total impact, 377 IG accounts reached
  • December 2024 — 2,453 total impact, 451 IG accounts reached

The full-year report told the bigger story. From April 2024 through April 2025, compared with the year before The Stoop took over (March 2023 to March 2024), Instagram reached 6,690 accounts, up from 575, and content interactions rose from 47 to 1,785. On Facebook, reach grew from 2,022 to 4,674 accounts, interactions from 61 to 1,189, and page visits from 1,713 to 6,491. Net follower gain rose from 43 to 181 across the two platforms: 34 to 95 on Facebook and 9 to 86 on Instagram. Over the year, the content drew 30,257 views on Facebook and 22,872 on Instagram.

Full Year vs. the Year Before

  • Instagram accounts reached — 575 → 6,690 (11.6x)
  • Instagram content interactions — 47 → 1,785
  • Instagram profile visits — 962 → 1,198
  • Facebook accounts reached — 2,022 → 4,674 (2.3x)
  • Facebook content interactions — 61 → 1,189
  • Facebook page visits — 1,713 → 6,491 (3.8x)
  • Net follower gain — 43 → 181 (Facebook 34 → 95, Instagram 9 → 86)
  • Views, April 2024 – April 2025 — 30,257 on Facebook, 22,872 on Instagram
