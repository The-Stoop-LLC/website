The Challenge

Dr. Tea was a beloved local bubble tea shop looking to amplify its brand presence and drive foot traffic — especially around key seasonal moments. As a small, family-owned business, they needed a marketing partner who could deliver big results on a lean budget.

The goal was clear: create buzz, build a loyal social following, and establish a scalable marketing foundation that could grow with the business.

The Campaign — Valentine's Day

#LoveIsBrewing Influencer Campaign

Local Influencers · On-Location Content Shoots · Paid Digital Ad

A Valentine's Day campaign that turned Dr. Tea into a must-visit date spot, built on influencer content shot on location at the shop and a paid digital ad.

The #LoveIsBrewing campaign exceeded expectations — generating 480,334 views, 651,244 impressions, 6,154 engagements, and 1,730 link clicks. The campaign drove 166 new followers during the promotion window, establishing Dr. Tea as a social media presence in Pittsburgh's food scene. Beyond the campaign, the full-service marketing proposal positioned Dr. Tea for long-term growth.
