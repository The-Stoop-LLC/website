SXP

Brand Video Production & Multi-Brand Partnership

Author: Mike Pernice, Founder · Last updated: September 2026

SXP is a sports performance clinic providing personalized 1-on-1 and group training for athletes at the high school, college, and professional levels. Located within X Shadyside in Pittsburgh, SXP operates as its own business venture. The Stoop produced two brand videos for SXP's website and social media presence, and brokered a multi-brand partnership between SXP, X Shadyside, and Lucid Juice.

The Challenge

SXP needed professional video content that communicated their training philosophy and facility to potential athletes and their families. As a sports performance clinic operating within X Shadyside, they also needed to establish their own distinct brand identity. The Stoop produced two brand videos and brought SXP into a multi-brand partnership that created cross-promotional value for everyone involved.

Talking Head & Website Intro

Website Hero Placement · Social Media Distribution

Two brand videos produced for SXP's website and social media presence — a talking-head video and a website intro with logo — giving a clinic operating inside X Shadyside a visual identity of its own.
