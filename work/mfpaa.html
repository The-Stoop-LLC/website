The Challenge

MFPAA is a critical community resource — providing emergency medical services across five communities. As a nonprofit 501(c)(3), they rely on community donations and EMS subscription memberships to fund operations, equipment, and vehicle purchases. They needed a marketing partner who could drive both donation revenue and subscription growth through targeted digital campaigns, while also strengthening their brand presence across the communities they serve.

Drive Donations from New Donors

Deployed 3 creative executions — 911, Impact: Chief Dell, and Impact: Christine — to reach new donors across the five communities, with custom audience targeting including lapsed donors, lookalike audiences, and high-net-worth professionals.

Capital Performance

  • $47,139 raised from 446 donors — average donation $105.69, up from $95.96 the previous year
  • 3,678 new website users — 79% of all site traffic during campaign
  • Aggregated CPC 87% below industry benchmark
  • Aggregated CTR: 0.99% (within benchmark)
  • Meta CPC 105% below benchmark
  • LinkedIn CTR: 1.87% (70% above benchmark)
  • Top creative: "911" — highest CTR across platforms
  • "Impact: Christine" — strongest LinkedIn engagement at 2.22% CTR

Across two campaigns, The Stoop delivered $50,349 in direct revenue — $47,139 in donations plus $3,210 in subscription revenue — representing a 6.9x return on ad spend. The campaigns drove 6,545 new users to MFPAA's website, generated 482 donors/subscribers, beat CPC benchmarks in both campaigns (69% and 87% below), and held CTR at benchmark. Beyond paid media, The Stoop delivered the supporting assets — a 30-60 second promotional video, 12 sizzle reels, two direct mail pieces, a social media template suite, and an After Effects motion graphics toolkit — giving MFPAA the tools for sustained community engagement.

FAQ

Questions About This Project

How much did the MFPAA campaigns raise? +

$50,349 in direct revenue across two campaigns: $47,139 in donations from the October 2025 capital campaign and $3,210 in subscription revenue from the August to September 2025 subscription campaign, a 6.9x return on ad spend.

Where did the campaigns run? +

Both ran on Meta (Facebook and Instagram), and the capital campaign added LinkedIn, using geo-targeting and custom audiences. Cost per click came in 69% and 87% below benchmark.

What else does The Stoop do for MFPAA? +

As MFPAA's annual marketing partner, The Stoop delivers paid media campaigns, video production including 30 and 60 second spots, direct mail design, social media strategy, and website optimization.
